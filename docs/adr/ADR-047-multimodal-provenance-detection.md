# ADR-047: Multimodal Provenance & Synthetic-Media Detection

Status: Proposed (Phase 5 Sprint 6 $\u2014 reserved at the entry gate; authored in full after the pre-code survey). Decision maker: Raman Sud.
Related: ADR-014 (observability), ADR-044 (multimodal input $\u2014 detect-in), ADR-045 (governed image generation $\u2014 emit-out), ADR-046 (multimodal content safety).

## Reserved scope (to be authored in full)

One synthetic-media-identity seam seen from both directions $\u2014 emit provenance on our outputs, detect synthetic origin on inputs $\u2014 consumed by ADR-045 (emit) and ADR-044 (detect).

- **Emit (outbound provenance).** Every image ADR-045 generates carries a **C2PA content credential** (signed, tamper-evident: generated by us, when, which model) plus an invisible watermark, attached as part of commit. The credential is a schema-validated structured output (P6).
- **Detect (inbound signal).** Image/audio arriving through ADR-044 is classified for synthetic origin. Detection is **probabilistic and adversarial**, so it is a **risk signal that raises tier / routes to the governed path $\u2014 never a standalone hard gate.**
- **Recorded in the trajectory (P3/P18).** Provenance emitted and detection verdicts are part of the durable, inspectable record.

This is the "distinguish AI images across the board" capability: provenance we can prove on our own outputs, and a synthetic-origin signal on everything we ingest.

L21: a provenance/detection conformance kit (credential emitted + verifiable; detection signal surfaced, not gating).

Principles (L12): P6 / P3 support; enables P4 (safety) and P17 (commit) via the risk signal + commit-time credential.
